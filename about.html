A2MD: Advanced Autonomous Materials, Manufacturing and Design

Bridging the Lab-to-Fab Gap in Advanced Composites & Extreme Manufacturing

The Industry Dilemma

Breakthrough polymers, self-healing vitrimers, and multi-stimuli responsive materials frequently fail during commercial scale-up. Conventional industrial processing equipment cannot handle extreme solid loadings (50–75 wt%), complex non-Newtonian shear thinning, and non-equilibrium crosslinking kinetics.

Laboratory chemists lack custom industrial hardware, while equipment manufacturers design for legacy thermoplastics and thermosets.

The A2MD Methodology

A2MD integrates predictive computational modeling (Materials Genome Initiative, ORCA DFT, LAMMPS MD) with tailor-built automated process hardware. We formulate non-Newtonian dynamic polymer slurries alongside the exact micro-extrusion systems required to print them under atmospheric or vacuum conditions.

Beachhead Focus: Lunar and orbital in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) — printing 50–60 wt% lunar regolith vitrimer composites for NASA Moon-to-Mars (LI-7) and commercial spaceflight primes (SpaceX, Blue Origin, Intuitive Machines).

Strategic Commercialization Roadmap
Phase 1 (2025–2026)IP protection, 60 wt% regolith-vitrimer validation, NASA/DoD SBIR Phase I proposals.
Phase 2 (2027–2029)SBIR Phase II pilot demonstrations & B2B predictive engineering consulting.
Phase 3 (2030)Full operational deployment of autonomous manufacturing suites for lunar & terrestrial infrastructure.
