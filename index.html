A2MD: Bridging the Lab-to-Fab Gap

Advanced Autonomous Materials, Manufacturing and Design (A2MD) overcomes the critical bottleneck where laboratory chemistries fail during manufacturing scale-up. We unite predictive computational design (MGI framework, ORCA DFT, LAMMPS MD) with tailor-engineered process hardware (custom PLC/Arduino extrusion systems).
